- **X1. '85% of (Instagram/Facebook) videos are watched without sound'** - Origin is a 2016 Digiday article citing a few publishers (LittleThings, Mic) and one agency, phrased 'as much as 85%'. - **X2. 'You have 1.7 seconds to hook someone' / 'people decide to scroll in 1.7 seconds'** - The real 2016 Facebook figure is average time spent with a piece of feed content on mobile (1.7 s vs 2.5 s desktop). It is not a scroll-decision deadline and not Reels data. Use B6 only with that wording and the 2016 date. (https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-releases-new-guide-to-help-advertisers-maximize-video-ad-performan/450818)
- **X3. 'Sends are THE single most important signal'** - Mosseri said sends per reach is 'one of the most important signals' and that sends are 'slightly more important' for unconnected reach; in Jan 2025 he named watch time, likes and sends together as the top three. (https://searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389)

'1 DM share = 15 likes, 1 save = 10 likes' / 'likes and follower count have near-zero weight in 2026'** - Agency blog (Pulse Advertising, Apr 2026) presents these as its own estimates with no Instagram source.
