## How to update it (Instagram app, about 1 minute)
1. Open Instagram and tap your **profile picture** (bottom right) to go to your profile.
2. Tap **Edit profile**.
3. Tap **Name**, delete the current name, paste the new name, then tap **Done / ✓** (top right).
4. Tap **Bio**, delete the current bio, paste the new bio, then tap **Done / ✓**.
5. Leave **Username** as it is.
6. Back on your profile, check that the bio shows on three short lines and the 📈 emoji appears.

Optional, same screen: if you have a pinned post from the old campaign, unpin it (open the post → ⋯ → Unpin from profile) so the profile matches the new direction once the first lessons are posted.
