## Every Reel (engine: lessons/engine.py in the publishing repo; scenarios.json holds the scripts)
- 1080x1920, 30–45 s, motion graphics only (no filming, no faces, no other brands' logos or accounts, no copyrighted music).
- Hook 0–2 s, complete in the very first frame: small letter-spaced audience label + big headline with a contrast/myth and a number.
- Lesson 2–32 s: why it works, real sourced numbers, one concrete example, one curiosity line mid-way.
- Payoff last 8–10 s: "That's what we build into every account we run." + our own published work (no faces) + end card
  "Made You Want It · Your product. An account worth following." + "First ad free · DM 'DAILY'".
- Voice: Kokoro af_heart @0.92 for every Reel (calm, warm, ~150–160 wpm with pauses after numbers). 75–110 spoken words.
- Karaoke captions for every spoken line (≤ ~8 words per line, key words/numbers turn red as spoken). Works with sound off.
- Quiet self-made music bed under the voice.
- Accuracy: every claim about Instagram comes from Instagram itself (Mosseri, @creators, help/transparency pages) or a major
  marketing publication; source recorded in scenarios.json and named in the caption. Never invent stats, results, clients or
  testimonials. Illustrative diagrams are labelled "Illustration". Our own numbers only when real.
- Caption = cheat sheet: hook line; 2–4 short lesson lines; "Source: …"; "This is exactly what we build into every account
  we run."; "Save this for your next post."; "First ad free · DM 'DAILY'"; 3–5 hashtags.
- One lesson per Reel. Never the same lesson type twice in a row. Refined, instantly understandable, never generic.

## Daily loop (no approval step)
- As soon as the Mac is on (7:35 run): make the next two unused scenarios, schedule 12:00 and 18:00 (America/Guatemala),
  then send the owner both videos + captions, and yesterday's summary (reach, watch time, saves, shares, profile visits,
  follows, DMs he reports) with a boost recommendation (only for a Reel already performing organically; he taps Boost; US$25/week cap).
- Check-ins 4 min after each post time publish and log permalinks; delete local assets one hour after each post.
- DM/comment questions → new "You asked: …" lessons. Fewer than 10 scenarios left → research and write 30 new verified ones.
- Sunday: weekly review — what the 1–2 hits share (type, hook, topic); make most of next week variations of the winners.
